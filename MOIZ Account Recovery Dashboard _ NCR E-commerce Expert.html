Why sales fell

1. Best sellers out of stock about 75% of the fall

Sep to Nov pace: monthly sales down ₹15.6L; ₹11.7L from ASINs with no stock, ₹3.8L from in-stock ASINs. Tripod fell from ₹10.2L (Jul) to ₹0.06L (Nov).

2. Ads stopped at the same time high

Weekly ad spend went from ₹1.65L (first 4 days of Oct) to under ₹5K by end of Oct. 66% of ad sales came from products that are now out of stock.

3. One product carries the account medium

The 110-inch eyelet screen (₹699) is 59% of Nov sales (13% in Oct). Conversion 1.3%; account average 1.08%; rating 3.9 from 346 reviews.

4. Returns eat the sales medium

Refund rate 18% in Aug to 36% in Oct, plus free replacements. Reason codes are needed to confirm the cause (size, creasing, damage or quality).

Advertising: what to do and what not to do

DO

  • Fix stock first; relaunch ads 3 to 5 days before it lands.
  • Start with the proven winners: Shree Tripod (22% ACOS), P1 Tripod (27%), P2 Wall (21%), Growz hero (24%), AD_READY (38%).
  • Rebuild into 8 to 10 campaigns by product group; each has 1 auto (close and complements only), 1 broad, 1 phrase, 1 exact.
  • Move the 42 winning terms (17% ACOS) to exact and phrase; start bids near the realised CPC (about ₹7.5).
  • Add negatives weekly: irrelevant terms (projector, home theatre, smart tv) and competitor ASINs.
  • Restart at ₹6K to ₹8K per day in total; raise 20% every 3 days while ACOS is under target.
  • Use 30% to 35% as working ACOS target until margin is known; test +25% to +50% top-of-search on exact winners.

DO NOT

  • Do not advertise ASINs with no stock or under 80% Buy Box.
  • Do not keep auto "substitutes" (118% ACOS) and "loose-match" (72%) on; they burned ₹79K.
  • Do not spread budget over 264 ASINs; 72% of advertised ASINs had zero orders.
  • Do not duplicate campaigns (216 ASIN PO A1 to A5 run at 100% to 200% ACOS).
  • Do not negate relevant sizes just because they have no orders; they have no stock. Lower the bid by 30% to 50% instead.
  • Do not raise budgets while refund rate is above 20%; it multiplies returns.
  • Do not scale hero ASIN ads until the Buy Box is above 90%.

Amazon warehouse (FBA) recommendation

Why move to Amazon fulfilment

  • Merchant-fulfilled sales were 56% of sales in July and only 3% in November; merchant stock ran out first.
  • FBA keeps Prime eligibility, faster delivery and better Buy Box odds. This matters for the 30% of hero traffic that misses the Buy Box.
  • Use FBA centres in the NCR and North cluster (Haryana and Delhi side) for your main buyers; confirm the allocation Amazon gives in the shipment plan.

How to do it

  • Phase 1: tripod, map and wall top 10, plus the hero eyelet. Phase 2: the next 10.
  • Keep 4 to 6 weeks of cover; send the first inbound at about 1.5 months of past demand (table below).
  • Check size tier, FBA and storage fees before sending, since screens are bulky. Strong packaging is needed to cut damage returns.
  • Keep a small merchant-fulfilled buffer as backup. Do not trust the 9,999 Flex quantities; verify real stock.
